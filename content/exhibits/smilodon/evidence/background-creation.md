# Background and preview creation

DUN created the quiet sky, ground, stones, and composition procedurally on 2026-08-30. No image-generation model was used. The background contains no animal, person, text, logo, watermark, footprint, or baked animal shadow. Posters and the thumbnail combine that quiet scene with a simple reconstruction silhouette of the hash-bound GLB; they are visual reconstructions, not scientific evidence. Final files use CC BY-SA 4.0.

Palette: pale cool-green sky, muted earth band, and low-contrast stones, so a land ice-age visitor can stand in a calm, readable stage.
