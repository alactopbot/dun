The official page identifies the pack as CC0 and describes six animated dinosaurs supplied in Blend, FBX, and OBJ formats for personal and commercial projects. The downloaded pack file was opened in Blender 5.2 LTS using `--disable-autoexec`. It contains one armature, one `Trex` animal mesh, flat-colour materials, an Idle clip, and five action clips. Three stale external image paths are not required by the visible flat-colour mesh and were removed before export.
