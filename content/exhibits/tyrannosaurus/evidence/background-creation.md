# Background and preview creation

DUN created separate 16:9 and 9:16 quiet Late Cretaceous earth-edge compositions procedurally in Blender 5.2 LTS with `scripts/assets/render_previews.py` on 2026-08-25. The existing muted Late Cretaceous palette was reused; no image-generation model was used. The background contains no animal, prey, person, text, logo, watermark, footprint, disaster, or baked animal shadow.

Posters and the 320×320 thumbnail were rendered from the final hash-bound GLB and `presentation.json`. The neutral first frame keeps the complete head, short forelimbs, feet, and tail inside the composition in both orientations. These previews are visual reconstructions, not scientific evidence.
