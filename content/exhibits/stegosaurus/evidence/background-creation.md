# Background and preview creation

DUN created separate 16:9 and 9:16 quiet earth compositions procedurally in Blender with `scripts/assets/render_previews.py` on 2026-08-25. No image-generation model was used. The background contains no animal, person, text, logo, watermark, footprint, or baked animal shadow. Posters and the thumbnail were rendered from the final hash-bound GLB and `presentation.json`; they are visual reconstructions, not scientific evidence. Final files use CC BY-SA 4.0.
